Indie Film Festival

100 volunteers are needed for next year's Indie Film Festival. Positions range from concessions to ushers. Each volunteer will receive free tickets.

Volunteers can also help welcome guests, answer questions, and guide people to the right theaters. There are roles for people with different interests and experience levels.

Festival shifts will be scheduled throughout the event. We’ll share shift details and expectations with volunteers before the festival begins.

Join us to celebrate independent films, meet fellow film fans, and help make the festival a great experience for everyone.
